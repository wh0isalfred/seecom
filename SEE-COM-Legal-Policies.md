If we cannot reasonably deliver to the address provided, we will contact you using the phone number or email supplied with the order.

The current standard delivery charge is:

- **₦3,500** for orders below ₦100,000; and
- **free delivery** for orders with a merchandise subtotal of ₦100,000 or more.

The applicable delivery charge is displayed in your order summary before payment. Discounts that reduce the merchandise subtotal below ₦100,000 may affect eligibility for free delivery.

## 2.3 Delivery Estimate

Estimated delivery time is **7–14 business days**.

Business days exclude Sundays and public holidays. The estimate begins after successful payment verification and order confirmation, not when an item is first added to the bag or when an unsuccessful payment is attempted.

The 7–14-business-day period is an estimate rather than an appointment or guaranteed delivery date. Delivery may be delayed by:

- public holidays or unusually high order volumes;
- limited-drop demand;
- courier or logistics disruptions;
- weather, road, security, or access conditions;
- events outside SEE.COM’s reasonable control;
- an incorrect, incomplete, or inaccessible address; or
- inability to reach the recipient.

Where we become aware of a material delay, we will make reasonable efforts to provide an update using the contact information supplied with the order.

## 2.4 Delivery Information

At checkout, you must provide accurate information, including your name, email address, phone number, street address, city, state, and any useful landmark.

SEE.COM is not responsible for avoidable delay caused by incorrect or incomplete information supplied by the customer. Contact us immediately if you notice an address or phone-number error. We cannot guarantee that details can be changed after processing or shipment has begun.

## 2.5 Tracking and Order Status

Where tracking details are available, the website may display the courier and tracking number. Order statuses may include Order Placed, Confirmed, Being Prepared, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Delivered, Issue Reported, or Cancelled.

## 2.6 Receiving an Order

Ensure that you or an authorised person is available to receive the parcel at the delivery address. Inspect the external packaging and item promptly after receipt.

Do not select “Confirm Received” until the parcel has actually been received. If the website displays “Report Issue” and you have not received the order, use it promptly or contact SEE.COM through WhatsApp.

# 3. No-Return and Order-Issue Policy

## 3.1 No General Returns

SEE.COM has **no general return or exchange policy**. Purchases are final except where the item supplied is wrong, damaged on delivery, materially defective, materially misdescribed, or another remedy is required by applicable law.

We do not accept change-of-mind returns, discretionary refunds, or size and colour exchanges. Customers must confirm the product, size, colour, quantity, delivery information, and total price before completing payment.

## 3.2 Requests We Do Not Accept

Subject to rights that cannot legally be excluded, SEE.COM does not accept a return, exchange, or refund where:

- the customer changed their mind;
- the customer selected the wrong size, colour, product, or quantity;
- the item does not fit as expected after the customer selected the size;
- the customer no longer wants the item;
- the item was bought as a gift and is unwanted;
- the customer found a different or lower price elsewhere;
- the item has been worn, washed, used, stained, altered, repaired, damaged, or handled beyond what is reasonably necessary to inspect it;
- tags, original packaging, accessories, or supplied components have been removed or lost; or
- the complaint concerns a minor colour or appearance difference caused by photography, lighting, or screen settings rather than a materially different product.

## 3.3 Valid Order Issues

Contact SEE.COM if:

- you receive a different product, size, or colour from the one shown in your confirmed order;
- the product arrives materially damaged;
- the product has a material manufacturing defect; or
- the product is materially inconsistent with its description.

Report the issue as soon as reasonably possible after delivery. Prompt reporting helps us distinguish a delivery or manufacturing issue from damage occurring after receipt.

## 3.4 Evidence and Inspection

To investigate a valid order issue, we may request the order number, customer contact details, photographs or video, the original packaging, and a description of when and how the issue was discovered.

The item must not be worn, washed, altered, repaired, or deliberately damaged while the complaint is under review. We may require the item to be made available for inspection or returned using instructions we provide.

## 3.5 Resolution

If SEE.COM verifies that the wrong item was supplied or that the item was materially damaged, defective, or misdescribed, we will provide an appropriate lawful remedy. Depending on the circumstances and stock availability, this may be a correction, replacement, or refund.

Where SEE.COM asks for a verified faulty or incorrect item to be returned, SEE.COM will provide reasonable return instructions and will not treat the matter as an ordinary change-of-mind return.

Refund timing may depend on Paystack, the customer’s bank, and the payment method. A refund is not the same as a chargeback. Customers should contact SEE.COM first so the order can be investigated and resolved efficiently.
